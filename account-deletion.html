PicChallenge Account Deletion

Request Account Deletion

Important: We're sorry to see you go! If you're experiencing issues with your account, please consider contacting our support team first. We may be able to help resolve any problems you're facing.

How to Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your PicChallenge account, please follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Send Deletion Request Email

Email our support team at:

lumiselfprojects@gmail.com

Step 2: Include Required Information

Please include the following information in your email:

  • Email address associated with your PicChallenge account
  • Reason for deletion (optional, but helps us improve)
  • Confirmation that you want to permanently delete your account

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

Please Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data That Will Be Deleted:

  • Your account profile information
  • Your voting history
  • Any saved preferences or settings
  • Your authentication credentials

Data That Will Be Retained:

  • Anonymous voting statistics (without personal identification)
  • Any data required for legal compliance

Processing Time

Processing Time: We aim to process all account deletion requests within 48 hours of receiving your email.

Before You Delete Your Account

Consider This: Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to:
  • Your voting history and preferences
  • Personalized content recommendations
  • Ability to participate in photo contests
  • Your account settings and saved data

Alternative Options

If you're considering deletion due to specific issues, you might want to try:

  • Contact Support: We can help resolve technical issues
  • Update Preferences: Adjust your notification and privacy settings
  • Take a Break: Simply log out and return when you're ready

Need Help?

If you have questions about the account deletion process or need assistance, please contact us:

Email: lumiselfprojects@gmail.com

Response Time: Within 48 hours

Website: https://lumiself.co.zw

Final Note: By requesting account deletion, you acknowledge that you understand this action is permanent and cannot be reversed. All your personal data associated with PicChallenge will be permanently removed from our systems.
